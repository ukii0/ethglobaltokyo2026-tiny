# Submission assets

- Logo: `logo.png`, 512 × 512.
- Cover: `cover.png`, 1280 × 720 (16:9).
- Screenshots: `01-home.png`, `02-customize.png`, `03-how-it-works.png`; unaltered captures of the running app. The customization screenshot uses an unsaved example name, Sunny.

Logo and cover were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using `public/images/sprout.png` as the character reference. Output dimensions were normalized with sips. Screenshots were captured through the in-app browser.

## Logo prompt

Create a square 1:1 app logo for Tiny Sprout. Use attached sprout as exact character reference: smiling white round pot, two gray leaves, thick irregular black ink outline, dot eyes. Center a single large entire plant on solid warm yellow #ffdf66 background. Simplify shading to crisp flat white and gray hand-drawn shapes. Generous 12% margin, all leaves and pot inside frame. No text, no border, no extra objects. Export square PNG, ideally 512x512. Save result to a local file.

## Cover prompt

Create final hackathon project cover banner for Tiny Sprout. Wide landscape precisely 16:9 ratio. Solid warm yellow #ffdf66 background. Reference is the exact app mascot: smiling white round pot, gray two leaves, thick organic black outline. Draw this mascot large on right half with a small black hand-drawn sparkle, lots of breathing room. Left half bold beautiful casual black handwritten brush script text exactly 'tiny sprout.' on two lines, beneath a small clean sans serif line exactly 'A little care. A little growth.' Balanced polished minimal graphic design matching playful black white gray and yellow webapp. No gradients, no UI, no other text, no blockchain logos. Keep all content within 10% safe margins. Save PNG.
